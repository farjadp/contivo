Most people who stop physio don't stop because it hurts. They stop because nobody told them what week three is supposed to feel like.

Someone books six sessions for a sore knee, does the exercises for ten days, feels a little better, and stops. A few weeks later the knee is back where it started.

That is why our first visit ends with a one-page plan. Not a list of exercises: a list of what to expect in week one, week three and week six, and which signs mean "call us".

Clinics talk a lot about techniques and equipment. Very few talk about the boring middle of recovery, which is where most people give up.

Clinicians: what do you tell patients about week three?
